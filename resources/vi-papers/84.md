A deep GP composes uncertain functions, but flexible conditional distributions may also need local latent inputs. This paper augments deep GPs with latent variables and develops importance-weighted variational inference for that construction.

Think of a latent input as allowing several plausible outputs at the same observed input. A local variational factor proposes that latent input, and importance weighting compares several proposals before taking a logarithm. The reusable pattern is
\[
\mathbb E\log w\quad\longrightarrow\quad\mathbb E\log\left(\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\right).
\]
Here the weights must be derived from the latent-variable deep-GP bound, including its inducing factors and analytically integrated terms. They are not arbitrary likelihood values from independent forward passes. The paper combines sampling with analytic integration to keep this calculation practical.

**What changes.** Both the model's latent-input construction and the inference objective change. This connects the expressive conditional modeling of deep GPs to the multisample principle of IWAE. More samples can tighten the bound, but they add computation and can weaken gradients for the local inference parameters. The following signal-to-noise paper addresses that particular failure mode.
