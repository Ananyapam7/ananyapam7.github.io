**The obstacle.** Mean field fixes dependence at zero, even when marginal approximations are good.

**Mathematical move.** Separate marginal distributions from dependence. For continuous coordinates with marginal CDFs \(F_j\) and marginal densities \(q_j\), a copula construction takes
\[
q(z)=c(F_1(z_1),\ldots,F_d(z_d))\prod_jq_j(z_j),
\]
where \(c\) is a copula density on the unit cube. The extra term specifies dependence; keeping \(c=1\) recovers independence. Stochastic optimization fits the augmented approximation.

**What changes.** The family \(q\), not the target or necessarily the ELBO. The decomposition separates **marginal fit** from **dependence fit**, a different route from invertible normalizing flows. The additional copula density and gradients must remain tractable.

Think of fitting the shapes of individual shadows first, then fitting how the coordinates move together. A copula supplies the second part. Dependence is only as expressive as the selected copula construction, and fitting a complicated dependence model can be much harder than fitting the original independent factors.
