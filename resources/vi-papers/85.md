**The obstacle.** Importance-weighted bounds can tighten while giving weaker learning signals for inference parameters—the issue already diagnosed by Rainforth et al.

**Mathematical move.** Study the structure of gradients for deep GP inference networks under importance weighting and adapt estimator design to the hierarchy. The relevant diagnostic is \(\operatorname{SNR}(\widehat g)=|\mathbb E\widehat g|/\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(\widehat g)}\); improving the bound is not equivalent to improving that ratio.

**What changes.** Gradient analysis and estimator design for a **particular structured model**. The reusable lesson is how a general gradient pathology reappears in a new application.

The problematic gradients are those of the additional latent-variable variational distributions. More importance samples can make their expected signal shrink faster than their noise. The paper adapts doubly reparameterized gradients to the deep-GP construction, preserving the objective while improving this estimator. It addresses training signal, not all limitations of the model or inducing approximation.
