Suppose \(\gamma(z)=p_0(z)\prod_i\ell_i(z)\), but each likelihood term makes posterior calculations harder. Expectation propagation replaces those terms by tractable **sites**, \(t_i(z)\), so that \(q_\phi(z)\propto p_0(z)\prod_i t_i(z)\).

**Remove, inspect, replace.** To revise site \(i\), first remove its current contribution:

\[
q_{\setminus i}(z)\propto\frac{q_\phi(z)}{t_i(z)}.
\]

This cavity distribution summarizes the other sites. Put the exact factor back to form the tilted distribution

\[
\widetilde\pi_i(z)\propto q_{\setminus i}(z)\ell_i(z).
\]

Project the tilted distribution into the tractable family:

\[
\phi^{(t+1)}\in\arg\min_\varphi
\mathrm{KL}(\widetilde\pi_i\|q_\varphi),
\qquad
t_i^{(t+1)}(z)\propto
\frac{q_{\phi^{(t+1)}}(z)}{q_{\setminus i}(z)}.
\]

For an exponential family, this projection matches sufficient-statistic expectations. A full Gaussian projection matches the tilted distribution's mean and covariance.

The removal step prevents counting an observation twice. Imagine revising a Gaussian approximation after reconsidering one unusually informative observation. EP first subtracts that observation's approximate contribution, then recomputes what its exact likelihood implies in the context supplied by everything else.

**What changes.** CAVI optimizes a variational factor against an expected log joint. EP updates an approximate **model factor** by matching moments of a local tilted distribution. These sites need not correspond to independent groups of latent variables.

Although each projection uses forward KL, EP is not simply global forward-KL minimization against \(\pi\). It repeatedly solves local problems whose targets depend on the other approximate sites. This distinction also explains the connection to [black-box alpha](#paper-57).

EP is attractive when tilted moments are tractable or cheaply approximated, including models where conjugate CAVI updates are unavailable. The difficult integration may merely move into the tilted moments. Iteration can oscillate, and cavity distributions can become invalid; damping site updates often helps. A Gaussian projection still cannot preserve separated modes.

[VMP](#paper-51) and EP share a local-message viewpoint, but their local mathematics and convergence behavior differ.
