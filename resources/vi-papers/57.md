[Expectation propagation](#paper-65) approximates individual likelihood factors by matching moments. Black-box alpha brings a related family of criteria into stochastic optimization, while sharing parameters across those factor approximations.

Write the target as \(\gamma(z)=p_0(z)\prod_{i=1}^N\ell_i(z)\), with prior \(p_0\) and likelihood terms \(\ell_i\). A tied-site approximation uses one function \(t_\phi\) to represent their average effect:

\[
q_\phi(z)=\frac{p_0(z)t_\phi(z)^N}{C_\phi},
\qquad C_\phi=\int p_0(z)t_\phi(z)^N\,dz.
\]

The prior and approximate sites are chosen in a tractable exponential-family representation. Here \(C_\phi\) is the approximation's normalizer, distinct from the target's \(Z\).

The paper minimizes the tied-site power-EP energy

\[
\mathcal E_\alpha(\phi)
=-\log C_\phi-\frac1\alpha\sum_{i=1}^N
\log\mathbb E_{q_\phi}
\left[\left(\frac{\ell_i(z)}{t_\phi(z)}\right)^\alpha\right].
\]

**Each observation asks how well its true contribution is represented by the shared site.** The ratio \(\ell_i/t_\phi\) measures that mismatch. The parameter \(\alpha\) changes how the expectation aggregates its values.

The connection to ordinary VI follows from
\[
\lim_{\alpha\to0}\frac1\alpha\log\mathbb E[e^{\alpha h}]
=\mathbb E[h].
\]
Taking \(h=\log(\ell_i/t_\phi)\) makes \(\mathcal E_\alpha\) approach \(-\mathcal L(\phi)\). At \(\alpha=1\), the method has an EP-like criterion, but tied sites make it different from ordinary EP.

**What changes.** This is a particular local-factor energy and a memory-saving parameterization, not simply a new symbol substituted into the global [Rényi objective](#paper-28). Monte Carlo approximates the expectations; minibatches approximate the sum over observations.

It offers control over the fit beyond reverse KL without storing a separate site for every datum. Sharing one site can be restrictive when observations have very different effects. Finite-sample estimates inside a logarithm introduce bias, and different values of \(\alpha\) can have very different numerical stability. Coverage of multiple modes can also assign probability between them.
